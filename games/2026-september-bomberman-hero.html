About This Game

Developed by A.I Co., Ltd. and published by Hudson Soft, Bomberman Hero reached Nintendo 64 in 1998. Unlike the arena-focused series staples, it is a single-player 3D platform game built around jumping and throwing bombs.

Bomberman pursues the Garaden Empire across Planet Bomber and beyond to rescue Princess Millian. Clear points reward efficient stage play, while vehicles and special stages vary the platforming.

Did You Know?

  • A solo detour: the campaign replaces traditional multiplayer battles with linear 3D platform stages.
  • Bombs on the move: Bomberman can jump and throw bombs directly at enemies.
  • A distinctive score: Jun Chikuma’s drum-and-bass-influenced soundtrack became one of the game’s defining features.

Critical Reception

Contemporary reviews praised the unusual platforming direction and energetic soundtrack while noting its departure from classic Bomberman battles.

GameSpotReviewThe contemporary review highlights the new movement and bomb-throwing mechanics and describes the soundtrack as techno and drum and bass.
