About This Game

Developed by Quintet and published in Japan by Enix as Tenchi Sōzō, Terranigma arrived in PAL territories on Super Nintendo in 1996 through Nintendo. The English-language version was never released in North America.

Players guide Ark through real-time combat and puzzle-filled dungeons while restoring the continents and helping life and civilization return to Earth.

Did You Know?

  • PAL English release: Nintendo published Terranigma in Europe and Australia, but it never received a North American SNES release.
  • A thematic finale: it is commonly grouped with Soul Blazer and Illusion of Gaia as Quintet’s creation trilogy.
  • A modern return: Clear River Games announced a new edition on August 24, 2026, expected in early 2027.
  • Dual composers: Miyoko Takaoka and Masanori Hikichi wrote the score.

Critical Reception

MobyGames reports an 85% critics average from 20 ratings. Reviews broadly recognized the game’s ambitious world-restoration story, action-oriented combat, presentation, and unusually large scope for a late Super Nintendo RPG.
