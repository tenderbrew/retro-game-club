About This Game

A child’s neglected old toys have rallied behind Huggy Bear. Command the newer toy planes, helicopters, and ground vehicles to reclaim rooms of a house, where pencils become missiles and erasers become bombs.

Missions mix combat with races, rescues, and household puzzles. Clearing missions opens more rooms; beating their target times unlocks room-boss battles. Defeated bosses join your force for the showdown with Huggy Bear.

Did You Know?

  • Perspective changes everything: A kitchen table can become a fortress and a flooded room can become a naval battlefield.
  • Recruit your rivals: Defeating room bosses builds the army you can bring to the final confrontation.
  • Before downloadable extras: Christmas Surprise and Summer Special missions appeared on Dreamcast magazine demo discs.

Critical Reception

IGN’s contemporary review praised the lighting, models, and imaginative miniature world, while noting rough edges and difficult missions. Hardcore Gaming 101’s retrospective likewise highlights the mission variety but criticizes the delicate vehicle controls.
