About This Game

Dr. Frank N. Stein’s patchwork hero Chuck D. Head pursues Max D. Cap after the villain scatters the parts of an island. Chuck punches from his chest, can recover a detachable skull to throw, and can drift through the air between jumps.

For its Western Genesis release, Vic Tokai transformed the Japanese Magical Hat adventure into a horror-comedy platformer with new characters, art, music, and altered stages—not just translated dialogue.

Did You Know?

  • A Halloween makeover: The Western game reworks Japan’s Magical Hat with spooky characters, altered visuals, and stages.
  • Float between jumps: Pressing jump again in midair lets Chuck hang and drift to stretch a leap.
  • A different score: The Western soundtrack differs from Magical Hat’s; an archived music dump credits Fumito Tamayama and Hiroto Kanno.
