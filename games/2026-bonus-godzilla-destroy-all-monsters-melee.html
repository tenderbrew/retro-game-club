About This Game

Pipeworks’ original arena fighter lets Godzilla and other Toho monsters trade blows across destructible locations. Energy attacks and grabs share the spotlight with a very practical weapon: the surrounding city.

The 2026 remaster returns with enhanced visuals, online multiplayer, eight locations with day and night variants, twelve kaiju, and an unlock system that lets players choose their unlock order. These are remaster features, not a promise about the original edition.

Did You Know?

  • The city fights back: Official remaster listings explicitly describe throwing skyscrapers and weaponizing landmarks.
  • A returning developer: Atari identifies Pipeworks as both the original developer and the studio behind the remaster.
  • Two eras, one bonus: The GameCube original dates to 2002, its Xbox port to 2003, and the remaster is scheduled for November 3, 2026.

Critical Reception

IGN’s April 2003 Xbox review praised the original game’s multiplayer party appeal and kaiju spectacle while finding the single-player mode less satisfying. This is historical reception of the Xbox version, not a review of the unreleased remaster.
